Eunjeong Lee is an associate professor at Chungbuk National University. My research is in equivariant algebraic topology. The fundamental objects of study in topology are manifolds, which are topological spaces that locally resemble Euclidean space near each point. Equivariant algebraic topology concerns the study of symmetries of spaces with algebraic structures.

My main research interest is to explore the relationship of equivariant algebraic topology with other areas of mathematics, such as combinatorics, equivariant algebraic geometry, equivariant symplectic geometry, and geometric representation theory.
